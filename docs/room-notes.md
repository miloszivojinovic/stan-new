## Entrance hallway + living + dining + kitchen (plan p1, 1:50)

Plan elements: console shelf 80 x 23 cm; mirror + seating 94 cm wide; shoe cabinet 145 cm wide; dining table
190 x 75 cm with six chairs; shelf / room divider 120 x 18 cm; kitchen island 192 x 80 cm; L-shaped kitchen along the
left and bottom walls; TV unit with AC concealer 293 x 37 cm along the bottom wall. A checkerboard balcony lies outside
the bottom-right wall.

Look:
- Floors: large-format beige tile in the hallway and kitchen; dark brown wood-look planks in the dining and living
  areas; geometric beige and gold rugs under the dining table and the sofa area.
- Walls: warm off-white to beige with a subtle texture; dark walnut vertical slat panels; a stone-look slab behind the
  TV and as kitchen backsplash and worktops (warm beige with gold veining).
- Doors and windows: dark walnut entrance door with a black lever; interior doors white with thin black diagonal
  inlay lines and black handles; white PVC windows (two on the wall behind the sofa) and a balcony door beside the TV wall.
- Hallway: floor-to-ceiling greige wardrobe / shoe cabinet with drawers at the bottom; mirror between walnut panels with
  a vertical LED strip on each side and coat hooks above; cream bouclé bench; small light-wood wall shelf by the door.
- Kitchen: matte greige upper and lower cabinets; walnut-clad tall column with two black built-in ovens; black hob;
  white sink; island with walnut-clad sides and bouclé bar stools with walnut legs.
- Dining and living: dark walnut table with black crossed legs and six bouclé chairs; open slatted shelf divider
  (beige and walnut verticals) with books and decor; L-shaped light grey-beige sofa, cream bouclé armchair, dark low
  coffee table; light beige TV unit with a linear electric fireplace and LED underglow; AC hidden behind a slatted grille
  above the TV; light curtains with a walnut side panel.
- Lighting: white LED strip lines in rectangular ceiling recesses (hallway, living, dining); round flush ceiling lights
  in the kitchen; large white drum pendant over the dining table; three cone pendants over the island; under-cabinet
  LEDs; LED underglow beneath the floating TV unit; vertical LED strips at the hallway mirror; warm wall-wash glow
  beside wall panels.
